Sunday prep

Cool rice and chicken promptly in shallow containers, refrigerate within two hours at 40°F or below. Freeze Thursday–Sunday cooked portions immediately; thaw overnight in the fridge. Reheat to 165°F. Keep hard-boiled eggs refrigerated and use within one week; add them after reheating rice. Pack meals in an insulated bag with ice packs. Prepare dry oats on Sunday and add milk the night before. Mix shake and Gatorade fresh.
