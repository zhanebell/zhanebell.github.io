Storage

Cool rice and cooked protein promptly in shallow containers. Refrigerate up to 3–4 days at 40°F or colder and freeze later-week portions immediately. Reheat leftovers to 165°F. Hard-boiled eggs keep about 1 week refrigerated. Add milk to oats the night before; mix shakes and Gatorade fresh. For fish, freeze portions for later days.

Nutrition coverage: rotate dark greens, orange vegetables, fruit, legumes, and oily fish. Fortified dairy contributes calcium and vitamin D. These menus improve variety but cannot guarantee every micronutrient target without a detailed nutrient audit.
