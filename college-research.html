College Research

Welcome to my College Research page! Here, you'll find an overview of my journey exploring various colleges and universities, with a particular focus on evaluating their financial, social, and academic fit for my goals. My reasons for applying to each school are listed below.

University of Washington (UW)

The University of Washington excites me because of its unparalleled resources and opportunities in research and innovation. As someone passionate about both mathematics and computer science, the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering stands out. Its emphasis on cutting-edge fields like artificial intelligence and data science aligns perfectly with my interest in integrating technology into problem-solving, such as in my STEM capstone project. The collaborative culture at UW, combined with the vibrant tech ecosystem of Seattle, makes it an ideal place to explore my academic and entrepreneurial goals.

Harvey Mudd College

Harvey Mudd College appeals to me because of its unique interdisciplinary approach to STEM education. I love how the Common Core Curriculum ensures that students build a strong foundation across multiple technical disciplines while integrating the humanities. This aligns with my own philosophy of connecting diverse fields to solve complex problems, like I’ve done with my math and computer science projects. The Clinic Program is especially compelling because it offers real-world, hands-on experience solving technical challenges for actual organizations—an opportunity I would embrace to expand my practical skills. Harvey Mudd’s intimate and collaborative community is the perfect environment to combine my love for math, coding, and creative thinking.

University of San Francisco (USF)

USF stands out to me because of its focus on social justice and community impact. I am passionate about using technology to make a difference, and the McCarthy Center for Public Service and the Common Good aligns with my desire to integrate my skills in coding and analysis into projects that benefit society. USF’s entrepreneurial spirit also inspires me—its programs in organizational development and ethical leadership would complement my interest in eventually building my own tech startup. The university’s location in San Francisco is another draw, offering proximity to the tech world while fostering a socially conscious mindset.

University of Hawaii

Growing up in Hawaii, I have a deep appreciation for the unique culture and natural beauty of the islands. Attending the University of Hawaii would allow me to stay connected to my roots while pursuing my academic goals. Being able to contribute to and learn from the local community while engaging in research makes the University of Hawaii an excellent choice for my higher education.
