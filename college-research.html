College Research

My exploration of universities that align with my academic and personal goals.

🏫 University of Washington

The Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering excels in cutting-edge fields like AI and data science—areas I'm passionate about. The collaborative culture in Seattle's tech ecosystem creates an ideal environment to explore my academic and entrepreneurial aspirations.

🔬 Harvey Mudd College

The Common Core Curriculum ensures strong interdisciplinary foundations, aligning with my philosophy of connecting diverse fields. The Clinic Program offering real-world problem-solving experiences is especially compelling for developing practical skills in mathematics and computer science.

🌍 University of San Francisco

USF's focus on social justice and community impact resonates with my desire to use technology for good. The McCarthy Center and entrepreneurial spirit appeal to my goal of eventually building a socially-conscious tech startup.

🌺 University of Hawaii

Staying connected to my roots while pursuing academic excellence. Ability to contribute to and learn from the local community while engaging in research makes UH an excellent choice for my higher education.
