What this model assumes

Home

Example shared two-bedroom rental. One household pays half the rent, with a private bedroom and bathroom and shared common spaces. Confirm utilities and lease terms.

Income & school

One part-time income and one projected full-time income. Irregular income is excluded from the baseline. Retirement deductions are assumed to be reflected in net pay.

Car & health

Example car-insurance and maintenance allowance, plus an estimated health insurance premium for one adult. Confirm actual costs.

Verify before signing: Actual full-time take-home pay, health insurance and payroll deductions (to avoid double-counting), rent/utility terms, shared lease liability, and car insurance.
